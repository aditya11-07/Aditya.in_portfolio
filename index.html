Robotics & IoT

Smart Hovercraft for Flood Rescue

Developed an ESP32-based remotely controlled hovercraft prototype designed for flood-rescue applications in shallow water and debris, motivated and mentored by Faiz Bhaiya. Powered by high-RPM BLDC thrust lift motors and electronic speed controllers (ESC). Directional thrust and throttle are wirelessly maneuvered via a self-made smartphone game controller interface with real-time response and emergency fail-safe protocol.
